# CV bullets

- Built a Python yield-curve pipeline that fits Nelson–Siegel–Svensson to 25 years of daily FRED Treasury data (6,308 days, 11 maturities); mean error 3.2bp in sample and 5.0bp on held-out maturities.
- Showed with PCA that level, slope and curvature explain 95.9% of daily Treasury yield moves, 95.5% out of sample, with slope and curvature changing shape at the zero lower bound.
- Backtested Estrella–Mishkin recession probits and DV01-neutral 2s10s trades strictly out of sample: the 10y–3m curve ranked recessions with AUC 0.76, while neither trade survived 0.5bp/leg costs (Sharpe −0.32 and −0.14).
